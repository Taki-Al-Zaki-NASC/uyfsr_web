1. Organization's Main Activities

What We Do

United Youth Forum of Science and Research (UYFSR) was established on 30 May 2025 with the vision of promoting science, research, innovation, and scientific awareness among young minds. Since its establishment, our organization has been dedicated to creating opportunities for students and young individuals to explore the world of science, expand their knowledge, and discover their potential through various educational and research-based initiatives.

We regularly organize and conduct various science-focused workshops, seminars, science fairs, Olympiads, competitions, and other educational programs. Through these initiatives, we encourage students to develop scientific thinking, analytical skills, creativity, and problem-solving abilities.

We also provide platforms for young researchers and aspiring innovators to present their ideas, showcase their talents, exchange knowledge, and gain practical experience. By arranging different academic activities and collaborative programs, we aim to inspire the younger generation to become more curious, knowledgeable, and actively involved in science and research.

Our Main Areas of Work:

* Science & Research Activities

* Workshops 

* Seminars & Educational Sessions

* Science Fairs & Olympiads

* Scientific Competitions

* Innovation & Project Showcases

* Research-Based Activities

* Academic & Youth Development

Our ultimate goal is to build a science-oriented community where young minds can learn, explore, innovate, and contribute to the advancement of science and research.

 2\. Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

The mission of United Youth Forum of Science and Research (UYFSR) is to introduce and promote the culture of research among students and young individuals. We believe that research is not limited to scientists or higher-level academics; it is a field of exploration that everyone should have the opportunity to understand and experience. Our aim is to inspire students to think beyond textbooks, ask questions, explore new ideas, and develop a deeper understanding of the world through scientific thinking and research.

We strive to create opportunities for young minds to discover research, develop their interests, enhance their knowledge, and turn their curiosity into meaningful ideas through workshops, seminars, competitions, Olympiads, and various educational initiatives.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a generation that is curious, creative, knowledgeable, and passionate about research and scientific exploration. We aspire to make research more accessible and familiar to students and people from different backgrounds, encouraging them to explore new possibilities, challenge existing ideas, and contribute to the advancement of knowledge.

Ultimately, we want to establish a strong research-oriented community where young minds are empowered to explore, innovate, share knowledge, and create a positive impact on society through science and research.
